# PLATO — Visual contract

**THESIS** — A private card room should feel tactile, social, and instantly playable.

**OWN-WORLD** — Swiss concert-poster discipline meets a warm casino table: confident black typography, restrained red signals, deep felt, walnut rail, brass details, and small character portraits.

**STORY** — Arrive, choose the table size, read the pot and your own hand, then pass or wager. Bot turns move briskly; a win closes the round and leaves the rest of the pot for the next one.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — Compact round header; an oval felt table with bot seats and a centered pot; the human hand below; a quiet turn indicator and a bottom action bar.

**FORM** — Portrait-first, responsive, no oversized “your turn” headline and no abstract geometric table. Keep the table immersive, the hand legible, and actions within thumb reach. Use Skia for the table illustration and Reanimated for the card reveal and tactile press response; add Rive when original animation assets are ready.

## Motion
Round entry deals two passes of cards from a visible central deck. Turns are locked until dealing finishes. Wagers stage their next state, slide a concealed third card from the dealer, pause, flip it, show the full three-card hand and outcome, then commit the new balance and turn. Respect reduced motion and cancel animations and timers on unmount. Shuffle, deal and reveal stages carry short card-handling cues from `src/features/table/services/cardSounds.ts`: four recorded takes per stage in `assets/sounds/`, preloaded eagerly and picked at random without immediate repeats, with one deal take fired as every dealt card leaves the deck, a reveal take as each dealt human card flips face up, a reveal take as a wager commits and the bettor's pair flips plus another at the deck flip, a reveal take as any player passes (including the human's silent pass, which opens no presentation), and a single shared reveal take when the winning round unmasks the remaining seats. The shuffle take fades out the moment dealing starts so cues stay on the animation's beat. Takes are mono AAC (.m4a, 96 kbps, 44.1 kHz), peak-normalized to −1 dBFS with silence trimmed by `scripts/normalize-cues.mjs`. Audio is decorative and must never block the choreography.

## Typography
